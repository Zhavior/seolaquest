# 🐉 Guild Lore: The Chronicles of SEOlaQuest & Lead Hunters

Before SEOlaQuest, outbound sales was a lone, repetitive grind in isolated spreadsheets. Reps spent hours copy-pasting links, logging activities without feedback, and battling lead fatigue.

Then came the **Guild Hall of Lead Hunters**.

---

## 🛡️ The Rise of Multiplayer Prospecting

SEOlaQuest transformed cold social listening into a cooperative RPG. Hunter guilds compete on weekly leaderboards, pool their Mana reserves for high-tier API radar sweeps, and celebrate converted bounties together.

## 🏆 Guild Leaderboards & Level Up Mechanics

Every action in SEOlaQuest grants Experience Points (XP):

- **Keyword Radar Deployed**: +50 XP
- **Lead Captured**: +150 XP
- **High-Intent Strike Completed**: +300 XP
- **Weekly Bounty Claimed**: +1,000 XP & Golden Crest

### Joining a Guild

Hunters can form alliances or apply to top-ranked guilds in the [Guild Hall](/guild). Compare your stats, claim group bounties, and climb to Rank #1.
