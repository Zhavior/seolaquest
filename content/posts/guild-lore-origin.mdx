# What the Guild Hall in SEOlaQuest actually is

An earlier version of this post described competitive guild leaderboards, alliances and shared Mana pools. None of that exists. There are no rankings, no other hunters to compete with and no shared guilds. The post also quoted a guild, lead totals and XP figures that were invented for atmosphere. They have been removed.

This is what is true today.

## Why the product talks like a game

SEOlaQuest watches X for the keywords you track and brings back public posts that look like someone wanting what you sell. Doing that every day is a chore, so the product borrows a game's vocabulary to make the daily loop easier to keep up: a hunter is you, a quarry is the customer you are after, Mana is the scan credit a search spends, and a quest is a small goal with a reward.

The words are a wrapper. The loop underneath is ordinary: find a post, read the source, decide whether to reply, and mark what you did.

## What earns XP, and what does not

XP is paid for a small number of real actions:

- **Engaging a lead that scored 60 or higher:** 25 XP.
- **Recording feedback on a lead:** 5 XP.

Scanning and discovery pay nothing, and neither does reporting a reply or a sale. There is a cap of 500 XP per day. Levels follow a fixed curve: level 2 takes 100 XP and level 10 takes 2,700 XP.

## What the Guild Hall is

The Guild Hall is a private journal of your own activity. It is not multiplayer. Nobody else sees it, and it does not rank you against anyone. Measures such as conversion rate are not tracked, so the Hall does not claim them.

## What is not built

- Leaderboards, rankings and guild alliances.
- Shared Mana between accounts.
- Any measure of how many replies turned into customers.

If those ever ship, they will be described here when they exist, not before.
