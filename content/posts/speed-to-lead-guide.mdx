## ⚡ The 5-Minute Decay Curve

### How Leads Go Cold — Faster Than You Think

Let's look at the raw data. MIT's Lead Response Management Study analyzed **over 1.25 million web-generated sales leads** across B2B companies. The findings are brutal:

| Response Time | Qualification Likelihood | Relative Decay |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **< 1 minute** | 🟢 Baseline (100%) | — |
| **5 minutes** | 🟡 ~80% | -20% |
| **10 minutes** | 🟠 ~40% | -60% |
| **30 minutes** | 🔴 ~12% | -88% |
| **60+ minutes** | ⚫ ~4% | -96% |

On platforms like **Reddit** and **X (formerly Twitter)**, the decay is even sharper. A user asking *"Anyone know a good alternative to [Competitor]?"* on r/SaaS will have **3–7 replies within 10 minutes**. By minute 30, the thread is buried under new posts and the buyer has already bookmarked two competitors.

### The Attention Window Is 90 Seconds

Social media intent signals have an **active attention half-life of ~90 seconds**. After posting a question or pain-point complaint, the user is actively refreshing, reading replies, and evaluating options. After 90 seconds, they tab away. After 5 minutes, they've context-switched to another task entirely.

**The implication is devastating:** if your team takes 10 minutes to even *see* the lead, you've already lost 60% of your conversion probability before anyone types a single word.

## 🔔 Setting Up Instant Slack / Discord Webhooks

Getting real-time notifications into your team's communication channel is the critical bridge between **detection** and **action**. Here's the step-by-step setup:

### Step 1: Create a Slack Incoming Webhook

1. Go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps) and create a new app.
2. Navigate to **Incoming Webhooks** → Toggle **Activate**.
3. Click **Add New Webhook to Workspace** → Select your `#lead-alerts` channel.
4. Copy the webhook URL (it looks like `https://hooks.slack.com/services/T00.../B00.../xxx`).

### Step 2: Create a Discord Webhook (Alternative)

1. Open your Discord server → Right-click the target channel → **Edit Channel**.
2. Navigate to **Integrations** → **Webhooks** → **New Webhook**.
3. Name it `SEOlaQuest Lead Alerts` and copy the webhook URL.

### Step 3: Wire It Into SEOlaQuest

Navigate to your **Keyword Radar** settings in the SEOlaQuest dashboard:

```bash
Dashboard → Keyword Radar → ⚙️ Settings → Webhook URL

# Paste your Slack or Discord webhook URL:
https://hooks.slack.com/services/T00EXAMPLE/B00EXAMPLE/xxxxxxxxxxxx

# Set notification threshold:
Intent Score >= 75  →  Instant alert
Intent Score 50-74  →  Batch digest (every 30 min)
Intent Score < 50   →  Silent log only
```

### Step 4: Test the Pipeline

Deploy a test keyword (e.g., your own company name) and post a matching message on X or Reddit. You should see the Slack/Discord alert fire within **5–15 seconds** of the post going live.

> 🐉 **Pro Tip:** Create separate channels for different intent tiers. A `#lead-alerts-critical` channel for 85+ scores ensures your top hunters never miss a high-value bounty.

## 🎯 The Quick-Strike Method: Pre-Drafted Value-First Outreach

Speed means nothing without **quality**. Blasting *"Hey, check out our product!"* in 10 seconds is worse than a thoughtful reply in 2 minutes. The Quick-Strike Method balances both.

### The 3-Layer Outreach Template

Every Quick-Strike response follows a **Value → Context → Bridge** framework:

```markdown
## Quick-Strike Template: Reddit / X / LinkedIn

**Layer 1 — Value Lead (2 sentences max)**
Directly address the user's specific pain point with a concrete insight,
statistic, or tip. NO product pitch yet.

**Layer 2 — Social Proof Context (1 sentence)**
Reference a relevant case study, metric, or shared experience that builds
credibility.

**Layer 3 — Soft Bridge (1 sentence + link)**
Offer an optional resource, free tool, or demo — framed as helpful,
not salesy.
```

### Example: Reddit r/SaaS Response

> *User posts: "Our team wastes 2 hours/day manually searching Twitter for leads. Any tools that automate this?"*

**Quick-Strike Reply:**

> Great question — the hidden cost there is actually worse than 2 hours: MIT research shows that manual scanning misses ~80% of high-intent posts because they get buried within 10 minutes of being posted.
>
> We ran into the same problem and ended up building automated keyword radars that push instant Slack alerts with AI-scored intent — cut our response time from 25 minutes to under 30 seconds, which increased our qualified pipeline by 4x in the first quarter.
>
> If you want to test the concept, here's a free radar setup you can try: [seolaquest.app/radar](https://seolaquest.app/radar) — takes about 60 seconds to configure. Happy to share more details on the scoring system if useful!

### Why This Works

- **Layer 1** proves you *understand* their problem before pitching anything.
- **Layer 2** establishes authority through *specific numbers*, not vague claims.
- **Layer 3** offers value (free tool) with zero pressure — the link is positioned as a *resource*, not a sales page.
